# RubyAlert trailer + overview: three storylines

One YouTube video, about 60–75 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Trailer energy first, then a quick overview of how it works. Facts come from README.md and the branding kit; nothing is invented.

**The one thing:** your phone says every payment out loud, in your language, with nothing leaving the phone.

## A. "The Shop Counter" (recommended)
- **Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain.
- A shopkeeper's counter at a busy hour. A customer taps pay, the shopkeeper glances down at a phone and misses it. The notification becomes a ruby pulse, then a spoken line in Tamil, then the same line in Hindi and English. The notification card morphs into the History list, then the Excel report, then the end card.
- **First two seconds:** a phone buzzing on a counter among customers, the notification "Rs 450 received" already on screen.
- **Beats:** hook (missed alert) → tension (checking the phone again and again) → reveal (RubyAlert) → proof (listen, language, dedupe, history, report) → payoff (three languages announcing in a row) → claim (nothing leaves this phone) → end card.
- **Look:** warm shop-counter ground, ruby `#E0103A` rationed to the alert pulse and the end card, real app screens.
- **Sound:** soft shop ambience, the app's own chime and TTS, light pulse music.

## B. "Type Carries It" (the bold one)
- **Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.
- Giant kinetic numbers (₹450, ₹1,200, ₹75) slam in on the beat, and each is spoken in a different language. Feature names hit as single words: Listen. Speak. Remember. Report. Real screens punch in only as proof.
- **First two seconds:** a full-frame "₹450" smashing in with the chime.
- **Look:** ruby and near-black colour fields, one big type family.
- **Sound:** beat-driven track, the chime as the snare.

## C. "Life Before and After"
- **Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
- Left: a person checking SMS, UPI apps and bank alerts across three phones. Right: one phone calmly announcing. The panes collapse into the right one, which then tours the app.
- **First two seconds:** the split screen, the left side frantic and the right side still.
- **Look:** cool grey versus ruby.
- **Sound:** a tense ticking that resolves into the chime.
